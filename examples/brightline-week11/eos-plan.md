# Brightline Freight — EOS™ Implementation Plan

Personalized plan derived from the RockSolid master action plan on
2026-10-05. Tempo: **Standard** (~52 weeks). Week numbers are
orientation, not gates — every step passes on substance (rubric +
team validation + explicit confirmation), not on the calendar.

Order follows Wickman's "traction first, then vision": structure,
Rocks™, Level 10 Meeting™, Scorecard™, then the Vision/Traction
Organizer™ (V/TO™), then processes and Everyone Has a Number.
