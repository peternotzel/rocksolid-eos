# GWC™ Assessments (per seat)

> **Status:** empty_template — to be filled in with the leadership team
> **Team-required:** yes (see `rocksolid/rules/team-required-tools.md`)
> **Source:** Wickman, *Traction*, Chapter 4 — The People Component

## How to use this file

For every seat on the Accountability Chart™, record a GWC assessment of
the person currently placed in that seat. The People Analyzer grid
captures the Y/N marks at a glance; this file captures the *why* behind
each mark, which is what makes the assessment defensible when a hard
decision has to be made.

## What GWC is

GWC is a three-part filter for whether a person is in the right *seat*:

- **Get it** — the person intuitively understands what the seat is for,
  how it fits into the business, and why it matters. Some people see a
  role instantly, some never do, and training rarely converts one into
  the other.
- **Want it** — the person genuinely wants to do this work every day,
  at fair compensation, on its own terms. "Wanting the title" or
  "willing to try it" are not the same as Want it.
- **Capacity** — the person has the bandwidth (time, mental, emotional,
  physical, and technical) to deliver the seat's 5 major roles at the
  level the business needs.

**All three must be Y.** Two out of three is a fail, not a partial
pass. A `N` on any axis means the person is in the wrong seat today —
even if they are a right person for the company overall.

## Rules (from the substance rubric)

- **Every seat on the Accountability Chart has a GWC block here.**
- **Every block has a Y or N on each of Get it, Want it, Capacity.** No
  "+/-", no "mostly", no blanks.
- **Each Y/N has a 1-2 sentence rationale** — not just a mark.
- **Any N triggers an action plan** (move person, enter Three-Strike
  Rule, or transition out).
- **Seat names here match seat names on the Accountability Chart**
  exactly.

## Action items (seats with any N)

For every seat where any of G, W, or C came back `N`, record the
decision and the plan. The three options are:

1. **Move the person to a better-fit seat** — if such a seat exists on
   the chart and the person has Y/Y/Y against it.
2. **Three-Strike Rule** — three coaching conversations over ~90 days
   with clear expectations and an explicit path to above-the-bar.
3. **Transition out** — when neither of the first two applies, or when
   the person is also below the People Analyzer™ Core Values bar.
