# Accountability Chart™

> **Status:** empty_template — to be filled in with the full leadership team
> **Team-required:** yes (see `rocksolid/rules/team-required-tools.md`)
> **Source:** Wickman, *Traction*, Chapter 4 — The People Component

## How to use this file

This is the structural map of the seats your business needs — not an
org chart of the people you currently have. Fill it out in a dedicated
leadership session, and build the whole structure before placing a
single name into a seat. Every seat gets exactly 5 major roles (not
tasks) that describe what owning that seat means. When the structure is
done, and only then, assign a name to each seat and run the GWC check
against the person placed there.

## Rules (from the substance rubric)

- **Exactly one Integrator seat.** Not shared. Not co-held. One name.
- **3 to 10 major functions** on the front line, with a minimum of 3
  core functions: Sales/Marketing, Operations, Finance/Administration.
- **Exactly 5 major roles per seat** — every seat on the chart, from
  Visionary down to the smallest sub-function.
- **Structure before names.** If names were placed before the structure
  was finalized, the chart is sculpted around existing people and the
  exercise has failed.
- **Visionary seat is optional.** About half of companies do not have
  one, and that is recorded as a deliberate choice, not a gap.
- **Sweet spot is 3 to 7 front-line functions.** More than 7 usually
  means some entries are actually sub-functions.
- **Revisit every ~90 days** as the business evolves.
