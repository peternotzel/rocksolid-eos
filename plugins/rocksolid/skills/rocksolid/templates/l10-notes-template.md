# Level 10 Meeting™ — Notes for {YYYY-MM-DD}

> **Status:** empty_template — copy into `l10-notes/{date}.md` each week
> **Team-required:** yes — the notes are of a live team meeting
> **Source:** Wickman, *Traction*, Chapters 6 and 7

## How to use this file

Copy this template into `eos-deliverables/l10-notes/{date}.md` at the
start of each L10™. Fill it in **live** during the meeting — the
agenda manager keeps it current on screen while the chair runs the
clock. The file itself is the meeting record; no separate minutes are
taken.

The seven sections below mirror the fixed L10 agenda. Fill each one
as the meeting moves through it. Keep the 90-minute hard stop —
anything unfinished flows to next week's Issues List.

## Rules (from the substance rubric)

- **All 7 segments recorded** in order — even if a segment is short.
- **IDS™ shows Identify / Discuss / Solve structure** against named
  issues. A bulleted stream of consciousness is not IDS.
- **Scorecard™ Review and Rock™ Review show status only** — bad
  numbers and off-track Rocks drop to IDS, not to paragraphs of
  discussion inside those segments.
- **Every To-Do has a single named owner and a 7-day horizon.**
- **Conclude segment includes the 1-10 rating.**
- **Meeting is held on the same day and time as the fixed L10
  agenda** (see `l10-agenda.md`).
