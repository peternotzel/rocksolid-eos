# Quarterly Planning Session™ Agenda

> **Status:** empty_template — filled in during and after the session
> **Team-required:** yes (see `rocksolid/rules/team-required-tools.md`)
> **Source:** Wickman, *Traction*, Chapter 7 — The Traction Component

## How to use this file

The Quarterly Planning Session is the 90-day heartbeat of EOS®. It is
**a full working day** (roughly 8 hours), **off-site**, for the
leadership team only, and **pre-scheduled far enough in advance** that
the "buildup effect" kicks in — the days before the session, unspoken
concerns and half-formed ideas accumulate in people's heads so attendees
arrive already primed. Springing the session on the team without warning
kills that preparatory effect and produces a shallower meeting.

Fill in the metadata block before the session. Work each agenda item
live during the day. Capture outputs into the linked artifacts as you
go — do not wait until afterwards, or detail evaporates.

## Non-negotiable session rules

- **Full day, not half a day.** Rocks™ work and Issues List™ work both
  need uninterrupted time; a half-day compression produces surface-level
  answers.
- **Off-site.** Office proximity leaks attention back to the business as
  usual. Pick a venue the team cannot casually walk out of.
- **Leadership team only.** Same seats as the weekly Level 10 Meeting™.
- **Pre-scheduled on the calendar at the previous session or earlier.**
  Do not schedule one quarterly at a time — schedule the next one before
  this one ends.
- **Devices off during working segments.** Break times are for messages.

      done/not-done (no partial credit)
- [ ] Scorecard™ in rolling 13-week view, current through the most

- [ ] Short-term Issues List and V/TO long-term Issues List both
      printed or projected
- [ ] Each leader has brought their own list of issues and candidate
      priorities for the coming quarter
- [ ] Full day blocked — no one leaves early, no one is dialing in for
      part of it
- [ ] Off-site venue booked; lunch arranged so the team does not scatter
- [ ] Next Quarterly Planning Session already on the calendar (should
      have been set last quarter)

## Agenda — full day (~8 hours)

### 1. Opening and team check-in — 30 minutes

Each leader shares briefly: one piece of good news, what has been
working and not working since the last session, and what they expect
to walk out with. Purpose: shift context from working *in* the
business to working *on* it, and surface anything that needs parking
on the Issues List before the agenda begins.

- **Facilitator note:** time-box each share.

### 2. Previous quarter Rock review — 60 minutes

Walk the previous quarter's Rock Sheet top to bottom. Score each
Rock binary (done or not done, no partial credit). For anything not
done, ask why in one sentence — typical root causes are
over-aggressive Rock-setting or a specific owner dropping the ball.
Capture learnings for segment 6.

**Target completion rate:** around 80%. Consistently much higher
means under-setting; consistently much lower means over-setting or
the cadence is breaking down.

- **Output:** previous quarter Rock Sheet archived with scores and
  learnings logged.

### 3. V/TO review — 60 minutes

Walk the V/TO page by page. The Vision page (Core Values, Core
Focus™, 10-Year Target™, Marketing Strategy, 3-Year Picture™)
evolves slowly — usually no changes at quarterly, only at annual.
The Traction page is refreshed every quarter.

For each section: is this still true as written, does anyone
disagree, does the Rock section need to be updated to the new
quarter. Capture edits directly into the V/TO artifact.

- **Output:** updated V/TO (Traction page at minimum); Vision-page
  changes flagged for the annual session.

### 4. Issues List long-form work — 90 minutes

IDS™ (Identify / Discuss / Solve) on the strategic issues parked on
the V/TO long-term Issues List, plus any new strategic issues
surfaced in segments 1-3. Work top-three-first by priority, never
top-to-bottom on the page. Some issues resolve into To-Dos for next
week, others become Rock candidates for segment 6, others stay
parked for a future quarterly.

- **Output:** updated Issues List with resolutions; candidate Rocks
  queued for segment 6.

### 5. Lunch break — 60 minutes

Eat together. Team-health compounding happens over the meal.

### 6. Next quarter Rock setting — 90 minutes

Run the 8-step Rock-setting process in miniature.

1. **Brainstorm** — dump 10 to 20 candidate Rocks onto the board,
   pulling from the Issues List, V/TO 1-Year Plan goals, and learnings
   from segment 2.
2. **Keep / kill / combine** — narrow to 3 to 7 company Rocks.
3. **Sharpen and date** — rewrite each Rock until it passes the
   specific / measurable / attainable filter with a binary done/not-done
   outcome and a due date (usually the last day of the quarter).
4. **Single owner** — assign exactly one name to each Rock. Never
   shared.

- **Output:** next quarter's Company Rock list.

### 7. Individual Rock setting — 60 minutes

Each leadership team member sets their own 3 to 7 individual Rocks,
inclusive of any Company Rocks they personally own. Walk the list
around the table so the whole team sees what each leader is committing
to and can challenge it before it locks.

- **Output:** new `rocks-q{N+1}.md` Rock Sheet with Company Rocks at
  the top and each leader's individual Rocks below.

### 8. State-of-the-Company communication planning — 15 minutes

Decide:

- When the next State-of-the-Company Meeting™ will be held (within
  the coming quarter, capped at 45 minutes)
- What gets shared with the full organization — Company Rocks only,
  not individual Rocks; wins from the previous quarter; any V/TO
  updates
- Who delivers it and who owns logistics

- **Output:** scheduled State-of-the-Company meeting on the calendar.

### 9. Closing — 15 minutes

Three things happen in order:

1. **Read back the new To-Do list** from today's IDS work. Every owner
   confirms their item is written down with a date.
2. **Expectations check** — did each leader get what they came for?
   Anything still unresolved goes onto the short-term or long-term
   Issues List.
3. **1-to-10 rating** of the session. Target is an average above 8.
   Lower triggers a quick diagnostic of what to fix next time.

Confirm the next Quarterly Planning Session date (90 days out) is on
the calendar before anyone leaves the room.
