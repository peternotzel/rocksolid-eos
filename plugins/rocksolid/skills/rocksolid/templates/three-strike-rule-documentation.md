## How to use this file

The Three-Strike Rule is the structured path EOS® prescribes when
someone on the team scores **below the bar** on the People Analyzer™
— either on a Core Value score or on a GWC™ (Get it / Want it /
Capacity to do it) axis. It is **not** immediate termination: it is
three rounds of conversation, each roughly 30 days apart, with
documented expectations and a re-assessment at the end of each
window.

The rule serves two purposes: it protects the business from impulsive
firings driven by a single bad week, and it creates a paper trail of
coaching and clear expectations before any termination decision —
which makes the eventual outcome fair whether the person improves,
self-transitions, or is let go.

**Most people improve before Strike 3, or self-select out during
the process.** Strike 3 terminations are the exception.

## Non-negotiable rules

- **Three rounds, 30 days each.** Not two, not five.
- **Each conversation is documented** — what was discussed, agreed,
  and the re-assessment date.
- **No skipping rounds** even if the gap feels severe.
- **No surprise terminations.** Strike 3 should never be the first
  time the person hears they are below the bar.
- **Confidentiality.** Visible to the manager and HR, not the team.
