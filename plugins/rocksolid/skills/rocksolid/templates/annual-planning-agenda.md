# Annual Planning Session™ Agenda

> **Status:** empty_template — filled in during and after the session
> **Team-required:** yes (see `rocksolid/rules/team-required-tools.md`)
> **Source:** Wickman, *Traction*, Chapter 7 — The Traction Component

## How to use this file

The Annual Planning Session is **two full days off-site** and is the
single most important session in the EOS® calendar. Day 1 looks
backward and inward; Day 2 looks forward — refresh the 3-Year
Picture™, set the 1-Year Plan, set Q1 Rocks™. Together the two days
run a full Vision-to-Traction cascade.

Fill metadata before the session. Work each item live and capture
outputs into linked artifacts as the day unfolds.

## Non-negotiable session rules

- **Two full days, not one, not three.**
- **Off-site**, preferably far enough that returning to the office
  is not a tempting option.
- **Leadership team only.**
- **Scheduled at the front of a quarterly cycle** so the annual also
  serves as that quarter's Quarterly Planning Session™.
- **Dinner together at the end of Day 1.**
- **Devices off during working segments.**

# Day 1 — Looking Back, Looking In

Approximately 7-8 hours of working time plus breaks. Focus is on an
honest accounting of the year that just ended and a structural health
check of the team, values, and chart.

### 1. Opening and team health check — 60 minutes

Each leader shares the year's three biggest accomplishments (from
their vantage point), their own biggest personal win, and what they
expect from the two days. Facilitator then runs a quick team-health
temperature read: 1-to-10 on how healthy the team feels and what
would move the number up.

### 2. Year in review — 60 minutes

Walk the year's Rock Sheets quarter by quarter: Company and
individual completion rates, wins to remember, losses to learn from.
Capture patterns — consistent ~80% is healthy, sustained deviation
signals a calibration issue worth naming.

- **Output:** year-in-review log with wins, losses, patterns.

### 3. Organizational Checkup re-administration — 45 minutes

Every leader fills the Organizational Checkup™ independently, then
the team compares scores component by component. Low-scoring
components become candidates for Rock topics or Issues List entries.

- **Output:** Checkup score logged; deltas from previous
  administration noted.

### 4. Core Values audit — 45 minutes

For each Core Value on the V/TO, ask: is it still true, are we
actually hiring/firing/reviewing/rewarding by it, is anyone on the
leadership team below the bar on it. Flag any drift for the Day 2
V/TO work — do not rewrite values inside this segment.

- **Output:** Core Values confirmed or flagged for rework.

### 5. Lunch — 60 minutes

Together.

### 6. Core Focus review — 45 minutes

Walk the Core Focus™ (Purpose/Cause/Passion and Niche). Is the
Purpose still the reason we exist beyond money? Is the Niche still
the one thing we are genuinely exceptional at? Have we been staying
inside the sweet spot, or chasing opportunities outside it?

- **Output:** Core Focus confirmed or flagged for rework.

### 7. People Analyzer for the leadership team — 60 minutes

Score every leader against Core Values and GWC™ (Get it / Want it /
Capacity to do it) using the People Analyzer™ grid. The bar must be
met or exceeded for every seat on the leadership team. For anyone
below the bar, confirm whether the Three-Strike Rule™ is running, or
start Strike 1 after the session and document it in the Three-Strike
template (not on this agenda).

- **Output:** People Analyzer scores recorded; below-the-bar leaders
  flagged for structured follow-up.

### 8. Accountability Chart review — 60 minutes

Walk the chart seat by seat. Is the structure still right for the
current scale, or has the business outgrown it? Every seat still has
exactly five major roles? Any seats that need to exist but don't, or
that should be merged or split? Structural changes require the
structure-before-names discipline — redesign first, then place.

- **Output:** structural changes captured; Accountability Chart
  version incremented if edits were made.

### 9. Day 1 wrap — 15 minutes

1-to-10 rating for the day. Confirm Day 2 start. Head to dinner
together.

# Day 2 — Looking Forward

Approximately 7-8 hours of working time plus breaks. Focus is on
rebuilding the forward-looking layers of the V/TO and setting Q1 Rocks.

### 1. Day 1 recap — 15 minutes

Quick walkthrough of Day 1 decisions and flagged items so the team
is aligned before the forward work begins.

### 2. 10-Year Target review and refresh — 45 minutes

Walk the current 10-Year Target™. Still specific, measurable, dated,
inspiring? Within 3 years of it — if so, roll it into the 3-Year
Picture and set a fresh long target. Rewrite only with real
conviction; wholesale rewrites should be rare.

- **Output:** 10-Year Target confirmed or refreshed on the V/TO.

### 3. 3-Year Picture refresh — 90 minutes

Throw out last year's 3-Year Picture and rebuild — rolling it
forward by one year is deliberate. For the new picture: future date
(3 years out), revenue target, profit target, 3-5 measurables, and
10 to 20 "looks like" bullets covering headcount, physical
footprint, operational maturity, systems, client and product mix.
Every leader must be able to mentally visualize the final version.

- **Output:** new 3-Year Picture on the V/TO.

### 4. 1-Year Plan setting — 90 minutes

Derive the 1-Year Plan from the new 3-Year Picture by asking: what
has to be true at year-end for us to still be on track for the
three-year version? Capture year-end date, revenue target, profit
target, measurables, 3 to 7 SMART goals (not 12, not 15), and
confirm the pre-drafted budget supports the new targets.

- **Output:** new 1-Year Plan on the V/TO; budget confirmed or
  flagged for revision.

### 5. Lunch — 60 minutes

Together.

### 6. Q1 Rock setting — 90 minutes

Run the Rock-setting process using the new 1-Year Plan as the source
of priorities. Brainstorm 10 to 20 candidates, keep/kill/combine
down to 3 to 7 Company Rocks, sharpen and date each one with a
single owner, then each leader sets 3 to 7 individual Rocks total
(inclusive of any Company Rocks they personally own).

- **Output:** `rocks-q1.md` Rock Sheet for the new year.

### 7. State-of-the-Company communication planning — 30 minutes

Decide when the next State-of-the-Company Meeting™ runs (within the
coming quarter, capped at 45 minutes), what gets shared (V/TO
highlights, Q1 Company Rocks, year-end wins), and whether a
separate kickoff event is needed for significant Core Values or
Core Focus changes.

- **Output:** State-of-the-Company meeting scheduled; kickoff event
  scheduled if needed.

### 8. Closing — 30 minutes

1. Read back the new To-Do list from any IDS™ work done across the
   two days. Every owner confirms their item.
2. Each leader states one specific commitment for the coming year.
3. 1-to-10 rating of the session. Target average above 8.
4. Confirm the next Quarterly Planning Session date (90 days out) is
   on the calendar.
