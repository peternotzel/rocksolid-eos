# Rock Sheet — Quarterly Rocks™

> **Status:** empty_template — to be filled in at the quarterly planning session
> **Team-required:** yes (see `rocksolid/rules/team-required-tools.md`)
> **Source:** Wickman, *Traction*, Chapter 7 — The Traction Component

## How to use this file

This is the **Rock Sheet** — a single landscape document that holds
both the company Rocks and every leadership team member's individual
Rocks for the quarter. Keep it as **one file**, not several. The
weekly L10™ Rock Review segment walks this sheet top-to-bottom in five
minutes, and that rhythm breaks the moment company Rocks and
individual Rocks live in separate places.

Once this quarter's Rock Sheet is set, a wall goes up: no new Rocks
may be bolted on mid-quarter. Ideas that surface during the quarter go
to the V/TO™ Issues List and are considered at the next quarterly
session.

## Rules (from the substance rubric)

- **3 to 7 Company Rocks** for the quarter (closer to 3 is preferred).
- **3 to 7 Individual Rocks per leadership team member**, *inclusive*
  of any Company Rocks they personally own — not on top of them.
- **Each Rock is specific, measurable, and attainable** in 90 days.
  One sentence of "done" per Rock.
- **Exactly one owner per Rock.** Shared ownership is diffuse
  ownership. Never a team, never two names.
- **Each Rock has an explicit due date** — normally the last day of
  the quarter (Mar 31, Jun 30, Sep 30, Dec 31).
- **Each Rock is binary-scorable** — done or not done, no partial
  credit.
- **One Rock Sheet artifact** covering company + individual Rocks.
- **No mid-quarter additions.** New ideas go to the V/TO Issues List.
- **Company Rocks are shared with the full organization** at a
  State-of-the-Company meeting within the quarter (capped at 45
  minutes).

## Status legend (binary — no partial credit)

- `[ ]` Not done
- `[x]` Done
