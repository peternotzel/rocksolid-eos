# Vision/Traction Organizer™ (V/TO™)

> **Status:** empty_template — produced at a leadership team off-site,
> not drafted alone
> **Team-required:** yes (see `rocksolid/rules/team-required-tools.md`)
> **Source:** Wickman, *Traction*, Chapter 3 — The Vision Component

## How to use this file

The V/TO is intentionally compressed to **two pages**: a Vision page
and a Traction page. The compression is a feature, not a limitation —
the constraint is what forces a leadership team to decide what
actually matters. Eight questions must be answered. Skipping any one
of them leaves a gap that every downstream tool inherits.

The V/TO is a **team** deliverable. A founder drafting it alone on a
Sunday afternoon produces an artifact the founder understands; a
leadership team producing it together in a one- or two-day off-site
produces an artifact the team has agreed to execute. Those are not
the same thing. If this file is filled in solo, mark it **DRAFT** at
the top and expect the phase gate to stay blocked until the full
team reviews and agrees.

**Visual structure to preserve:**

- **Page 1 — Vision page** — two columns. The left column stacks
  Core Values, Core Focus™, 10-Year Target™, and Marketing Strategy
  (slow-changing identity). The right column holds the 3-Year
  Picture™ (concrete forward snapshot). The left evolves slowly;
  the right gets rebuilt annually.
- **Page 2 — Traction page** — sequential sections: 1-Year Plan,
  Quarterly Rocks™, and the long-term Issues List™.

## Rules (from the substance rubric)

- **All eight questions answered**, not a subset.
- **3 to 7 Core Values**, each concrete enough to decide a hire/fire
  question with. No generic one-word abstractions.
- **Core Focus has both sub-parts** — a Purpose/Cause/Passion
  statement and a Niche statement, each in a few words.
- **10-Year Target is a single goal** with a date and a measurable
  number. Not a list. Not "become the leader in our space."
- **Target Market is filterable** — specific enough along demographic,
  geographic, and psychographic axes that a real prospect list could
  be built from it.
- **Exactly three Uniques™**, and no single competitor can honestly
  claim all three together.
- **Proven Process™ is named, illustrated, and has 3 to 7 major
  steps.** An unnamed process does not work in a sales conversation.
- **Guarantee is either stated concretely or an explicit decision not
  to have one is recorded.** Roughly half of companies don't carry
  one — that's fine, but the decision must be intentional.
- **3-Year Picture has all fields:** future date, revenue, profit,
  measurables, and 10 to 20 "looks like" bullets.
- **1-Year Plan has 3 to 7 SMART goals** (not 12 or 15) plus a
  realistic budget that supports the revenue and profit targets.
- **Quarterly Rocks on the V/TO match the current Rock Sheet** —
  canonical statement of what this quarter is about.
- **Long-term Issues List has entries** — an empty list usually
  means the team isn't surfacing horizon concerns.
- **Team authorship is recorded** — if solo, the file is DRAFT and
  the phase gate is blocked.

## Sharing the vision with the whole organization

Once the V/TO exists, half the work of the Vision Component is done.
The other half is repeatedly communicating it to everyone. Leaders
who assume employees "got it" the first time consistently find that
they didn't.

- **Kickoff event scheduled for:** _(unveiling of the finalized
  V/TO, especially Core Values, with Q&A for the full workforce)_
- **Quarterly State-of-the-Company meeting:** capped at 45 minutes,
  audience is the entire organization, refreshes the V/TO and
  announces current-quarter Rocks.
- **Quarterly leadership review of the V/TO:** part of every
  quarterly planning session.
