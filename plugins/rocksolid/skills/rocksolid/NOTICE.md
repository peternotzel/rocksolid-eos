## Source material

This skill guides users through concepts, tools, and terminology from the
Entrepreneurial Operating System (EOS™) as described in *Traction: Get a Grip
on Your Business* by Gino Wickman (BenBella Books, Expanded Edition).

## Paraphrase policy

All explanations, frameworks, criteria, and guidance in this skill are
original paraphrases and summaries written independently from the source.
Direct quotations from the book are not reproduced. Wickman's specific
examples, case studies, metaphors, and client stories are not copied —
they are replaced with original illustrations or omitted entirely.

Facts, numbers, and structural rules (e.g., "3-7 Core Values", "90-minute
L10 Meetings", "5 major roles per seat") are stated as facts — these are
non-copyrightable.

The implementation order (Accountability Chart → Rocks → Meeting Pulse →
Scorecard → V/TO → Process Documenter → Everyone Has a Number) is a
sequence derived from Wickman's Chapter 9 recommendation — a process, not
a text.
